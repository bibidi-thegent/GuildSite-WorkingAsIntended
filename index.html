Why Raid With Us

Stability

Same core roster across multiple tiers. We don't rebuild every season — we refine. Officers who show up, a bench that gets real playtime, and zero tier-end collapses.

Efficiency

Two focused nights, not five sloppy ones. Assignments posted before pull one, logs reviewed between raids, and consumables covered by the guild bank.

Community

M+ keys every night of the week, active Discord, and people who actually like each other. Progression matters — but so does wanting to log in.
